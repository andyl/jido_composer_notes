Research notes

Carl Hewitt

Papers and proposals on very large agent systems

Notes compiled October 20, 2026 · Andy Leak

Summary

Carl Hewitt (1944–2022), creator of the Actor Model, wrote extensively about very large, decentralized agent systems. Late in life he proposed national-scale projects to build them.

1Late-career proposals (2017–2022)

Scalable Intelligent Systems by 2025

He gave this as a lecture series and as seminars, including Edinburgh LFCS in September 2018 and the St Andrews Distinguished Lecture Series.

  • He argued that building the technology stack he outlined could deliver Scalable Intelligent Systems by 2025.
  • Citadels were the organizations of people and IoT devices in the architecture. Their purpose was trustworthiness, resilience, and performance with no single point of failure.
  • He held that there is no computer-only solution, because people are fundamental to a Scalable Intelligent System.
  • The technology stack had these layers:
  1. Experiences (hologlasses)
  2. Matrix (discourse, rhetoric, narration)
  3. Citadels
  4. Massive Inconsistency Robust Ontology
  5. Actor Services
  6. Actor Many Cores

Top of the stack first.

Sources: web.inf.ed.ac.uk/node/3027 · Edinburgh LFCS seminar, 12 September 2018 · St Andrews DLS

Project Liftoff: Reusable Secure Intelligent Systems by 2030

  • This was his proposed project for education, science, and technology to implement universal Reusable Secure Intelligent Systems (RSIS) by 2030.
  • He framed it as a national competition, noting that China was racing to develop its own such systems.
  • This is the closest match found to the "national applications" framing of the original question.

Source: codesync.global/speaker/carl-hewitt

Code Mesh LDN 2018: "Tactics and strategies for scalable robust intelligent systems"

  • The tactics part covered using Erlang and Elixir to implement scalable, robust concurrency.
  • The strategies part covered implementing universal reusable secure intelligent systems.

Source: codesync.global/speaker/carl-hewitt
